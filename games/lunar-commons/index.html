Mission log
    Four operators · one south pole

    Who is at the table?

    Each round, every operator decides how to fund its work on the Moon: alone, through the commons, or in a consortium. That choice decides who sees the survey data, who can use the power towers, and whether the region ends in stagnation, monopoly, or sustained growth.

    An educational simulation of resource-governance choices. Credits are fictional, crater positions are stylized, and nothing here is financial or investment advice.

    How the exercise works

    The board is a 10 × 8 grid over the lunar south pole. The Sun never climbs more than about 1.5° above the horizon here, so crater floors sit in permanent shadow and hold water ice, while rims and massifs catch near-constant sunlight. You can see the terrain, but a tile's exact resources are unknown until someone surveys it.

    A round

    • Event. One event is drawn (launch window, solar particle event, supply shortage…).
    • Funding. Each operator picks a funding model for the round. Choices are made in turn order and are visible to those who choose later.
    • Operations. Each operator takes 2 actions.
    • Production. Each outpost yields ice + sun + 1 per power tower within one tile that serves it (max +3), minus 1 credit upkeep.

    A consortium needs at least two members in the same round; a lone consortium bidder operates alone. While anyone funds the commons, Earth agencies add a 3-credit grant each round. Whenever the pool reaches 8 credits it builds a public relay tower (white disc) where it serves the most different operators.

    Actions

    • Survey (2): reveal a tile's ice, sun and terrain roughness.
    • Land outpost (5): claim an empty tile. Landing blind on rough terrain fails 35% of the time. An outpost landed while operating alone declares a safety zone: no other operator may land on the four tiles bordering it.
    • Power tower (6): build on a tile you hold. It boosts every eligible outpost on and around it.

    How it ends

    • Stagnation: the development index stays under the target for the game length.
    • Monopolization: one operator holds more than 45% of total net worth.
    • Sustained growth: the region is developed and no one dominates.

    Net worth is credits + 4 per outpost + 5 per power tower you own. Everyone at the table shares the regional outcome, so a personal win inside a monopoly or a stagnant region is a hollow one.

    Controls

    • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Press R to reset the view.
    • Pick an action, then click a tile. On touch screens, tap a tile and confirm.
